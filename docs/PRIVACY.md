# Plates — Privacy Policy

**Effective date: August 10, 2026**

Plates is built to be private by design. The short version: **your data stays
on your iPhone, and we collect nothing.**

## What Plates stores

Your restaurant collection — restaurants, visits, amounts, receipt photos,
food photos, notes, and your optional profile name and photo — is stored
locally on your device. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it.

## What Plates does NOT do

- No accounts. You never sign in.
- No analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.
- No servers operated by the developer. Nothing you scan or type is
  transmitted to us — we could not read your data if we wanted to.

## On-device processing

Receipt images are read using Apple's on-device text recognition, and
personalized suggestions are generated using Apple's on-device intelligence.
These run entirely on your iPhone.

## Permissions Plates asks for

- **Camera** — only to scan receipts. Images are processed on-device.
- **Photos** — only when you choose to attach or scan an existing photo.
  Plates uses Apple's out-of-process photo picker and can only access the
  photos you explicitly select.
- **Location (while using)** — only to rank nearby restaurants when matching
  a receipt. Your location is not stored or transmitted by the developer.

## Apple services

Some features send limited queries to Apple services under Apple's own
privacy policy: restaurant and city search use Apple Maps, and purchases are
processed by the App Store. The developer receives no personal information
from these services.

## Michelin data

Michelin Guide star information is bundled inside the app as an offline
dataset. No third-party service receives your activity.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same
URL with a new effective date.
